English

YojanaMitra helps organize scholarship, job and internship information. Records may be incomplete, stale or unavailable. Eligibility results are rule-based estimates from the information stored in the app; they do not guarantee eligibility, selection or payment. AI answers can be wrong and are informational only.

Always verify current rules, deadlines, amounts, documents and application instructions on the official portal. YojanaMitra does not submit applications or sign in to public portals for you. You are responsible for your application and for keeping your account credentials safe.
